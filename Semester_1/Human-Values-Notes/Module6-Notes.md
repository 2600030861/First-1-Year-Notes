# CO6: Existence, Natural Acceptance and Ethical Human Conduct

## 1. Co-Existence in Existence

**Existence** = **units submerged in all-pervading space.**

| | **Units (Nature)** | **Space** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Size | Limited | All-pervading, unlimited |
| Activity | Active | No activity |
| Role | Self-organized, energized; recognize and fulfill relationships | Provides the environment for self-organization; background for all units |
| Energy | Energized | Constant energy; transparent |

### The Four Orders of Nature

| Order | Includes | Characteristic activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Material** | Soil, minerals, air, water | Physical activities |
| **Pranic** | Plants, flora, biological growth | Life processes (respiration, growth) |
| **Animal** | Animal bodies | Conscious activities |
| **Human** | Human body + knowledge, physical facilities, consciousness, understanding | Understanding-based living |

### Recyclability and Self-Regulation
- **Recyclability:** material units transform through natural processes and return to their original form (water, oxygen and nitrogen cycles).
- **Self-regulation:** nature's inherent balance controls plant and animal populations so all breeds continue to **co-exist without depleting resources.**
- **Role of education and Sanskar:** helps humans move from unguided living to **definite human conduct**, so they fit into nature's self-regulating harmony.

## 2. Natural Acceptance of Human Values

- Human values are **inherent in existence**. They are **discovered through self-exploration, not imposed.**
- **Do not enforce values through:**
  - **Fear** (Bhaya)
  - **Greed** (Pralobhana)
  - **Blind faith/belief** (Astha)
- The right basis is **Right Understanding**, so that values are *naturally acceptable*.

### Progression of Self-Realization

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Swatva** (Innate identity) | Natural acceptance that is **innate, invariant, universal** |
| **Swatantrata** (Self-organized living) | Realizing harmony within oneself and living by it |
| **Swarajya** (Self-expression in society) | Working to ensure harmony in the larger societal order |

### Implications of value-based living

| Level | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| **Individual** | Peace, happiness, contentment, bliss, perseverance, bravery, generosity |
| **Family** | Mutual fulfillment, prosperity, sustained joint-family structures, respect for all regardless of age, gender, caste, race or status |
| **Society** | Fearlessness; holistic systems of education, health, justice, production, exchange; global harmony |
| **Nature** | Co-existence of all units, seasonal balance, resource preservation, a **Universal Human Order** |

## 3. Utility Value vs. Artistic Value

| | **Utility Value** | **Artistic Value** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaning | Using physical facilities to **nurture, protect and provide** for the body | Preserving and enhancing the utility of a facility (**right utilization**) |

| Item | Utility value | Artistic value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pen** | Writing | Leak-proof cap, comfortable grip |
| **Shirt** | Protects body from weather | Buttons, proper sizing for comfort |
| **Food** | Nurtures the body | Cooking so it is digestible, serving it properly |

> **Key principle:** Artistic value exists **only when utility value is already present.** Without utility, art has no scope.

## 5. Definiteness of Ethical Human Conduct

### 5.1 Concept of Innateness (Humanness)
- Everything in nature has a definite innate character: a mango tree has "mango-ness", iron has "iron-ness", a cow has "cow-ness".
- Human beings have a definite innate character called **Humanness (Manavta).**
- Right Understanding gained by self-exploration reveals **Ethical Human Conduct (Manaviya Acharan)**, which is **universal and invariant** across all humans.
- Debates about what is "ethical" arise only from **lack of Right Understanding.** With it, conduct becomes definite.

### 5.2 Three Dimensions of Definite Ethical Human Conduct (Nischit Manaviya Acharan)

```
Ethical Human Conduct = Values (Mulya) + Policy (Niti) + Character (Charitra)
```

#### I. Values (Mulya)
- Universal human values come from **realization and understanding of the harmony** at all levels of existence.
- Once existence is understood, a person's imagination naturally turns toward fulfilling these values through definite participation.
- Categories: **Established values** and **Expressed values** (Section 4).

#### II. Policy (Niti)
- The **strategic framework or plan** to protect and enrich physical, mental and societal resources in line with Right Understanding.

#### III. Character (Charitra)
The actual **external manifestation of consciousness in daily living**, with three pillars:

| Pillar | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Rightfully acquired wealth** (Swa-Dhan) | Acquiring resources **without exploitation** (*Shoshan Mukt*) |
| **Chastity in relationships** (Swa-Nari / Swa-Purush) | Loyalty and fidelity in marital relationships |
| **Compassionate behavior and work** (Dayapurn Vyavahar and Karya) | Taking responsibility to **fill the gaps created by ignorance**, in human relationships and in work with nature |

## 6. Human Conduct: Animal vs. Human Consciousness

### Living with Animal Consciousness (lack of Right Understanding)
Common root: a **feeling of inability to take care of one's own body.**

| Trait | How it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Wretchedness** (Deenata) | Helplessness and total dependence on others |
| **Cunningness** (Heenata) | Beguiling or tricking others |
| **Cruelty** (Kroorata) | Violence and forcefulness against others |

### Living with Human Consciousness (guided by Right Understanding)

| Trait | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Perseverance** (Dheerata) | Assurance that the living solution lies in understanding and living in harmony across all 4 levels; steady commitment without perturbation |
| **Bravery** (Veerata) | Commitment to help others develop right understanding and live in harmony |
| **Generosity** (Udarata) | Readiness to invest one's self, body and physical facilities to help others gain right understanding |

**Pairing to remember:**
Wretchedness leads to **Perseverance**, Cunningness leads to **Bravery**, Cruelty leads to **Generosity**.

### Completion states in a human being
- **Kriyapurnata:** completion of **right understanding.**
- **Acharanpurnata:** the ability to **live** with complete understanding.

## 7. Profession and Its Role in the Larger Order

**Profession:** a significant domain of human activity for participating in the larger order (society and nature), usually centered on production activities.

**Dual role**
1. Provides physical facilities (**livelihood**) for oneself and one's family.
2. Acts as a **channel for meaningful participation and service** to the larger order.

**Ethical conduct in profession** means using professional skills to fulfill the **comprehensive human goal**, the happiness and prosperity of all.

**Professional education:** technical education (e.g., engineering) gives skills, but **ethical competence** is needed to direct those skills toward human welfare.

---

## 8. Five Competencies in Professional Ethics

| # | Competence | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Clarity on goals** | Understanding the comprehensive human goal (*Samadhana-Samriddhi-Abhaya-Sah-astitva*), fulfilled through a universal human order from family to world family |
| 2 | **Confidence and harmony** | Self-confidence and faith in the prevailing harmony, co-existence and self-regulation of existence |
| 3 | **Mutually fulfilling behavior** | Ability to build mutually fulfilling relationships, with clarity on ethical conduct, for sustainable individual and collective well-being |
| 4 | **Mutually enriching interaction with nature** | Assessing physical needs accurately and producing facilities through eco-friendly, harmonious production |
| 5 | **Actualization** | Applying and living right understanding in everyday real-life situations |

**The four parts of the comprehensive human goal**
| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Samadhana** | Resolution / peace (individual) |
| **Samriddhi** | Prosperity (family) |
| **Abhaya** | Fearlessness (society) |
| **Sah-astitva** | Co-existence (nature) |

## 9. Present Unethical Practices

- Corruption at multiple levels
- Tax evasion and misuse of public funds
- Misleading advertising, propaganda, aggressive sales tactics
- Cut-throat competition and exploitation of consumer weaknesses
- Adulteration, spurious production, hoarding, over-charging
- Endangering public safety and health

**Underlying cause:** lack of Right Understanding and a misplaced goal (accumulation, profit and consumption). The remedy is Right Understanding, which makes conduct definite and ethical.

## CO6 Quick Revision
- Existence = **units in space**; four orders: Material, Pranic, Animal, Human.
- Values are **discovered by self-exploration**, never enforced by fear, greed or blind faith.
- Swatva, then Swatantrata, then Swarajya.
- **Utility** comes first; **artistic value** depends on it.
- Ethical Human Conduct = **Values + Policy + Character.**
- Character has three pillars: rightly acquired wealth, chastity, compassionate behavior.
- Animal consciousness (**Deenata, Heenata, Kroorata**) vs. human consciousness (**Dheerata, Veerata, Udarata**).
- Five professional-ethics competencies: goals, harmony, fulfilling behavior, nature interaction, actualization.
