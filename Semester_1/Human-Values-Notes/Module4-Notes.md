# CO4: Family, Society and Harmony in Relationships

## 1. Family as the Basic Unit of Interaction

**Family (Parivara):** a group of individuals living in a relationship of **mutual fulfillment**, so that each member has **Right Understanding** and **Prosperity**.

**Why the family matters**
- **Foundation of society:** it is the basic unit of human interaction and carries human tradition forward from one generation to the next.
- **Natural laboratory:** the family is where we *understand, practice, evaluate and fulfill* harmony in relationships. We learn it here first and then extend it to society and nature.
- **Web of dependency:** life depends on far more than parents and siblings. It also depends on extended family, friends, teachers, and service providers (grocery shopkeeper, milkman, helper, etc.).

## 3. Values in Relationship

The notes build up a chain of values. Each one grows out of the earlier ones.

```
Trust -> Respect -> Affection -> Care / Guidance -> Reverence -> Glory / Gratitude -> Love
```

### 3.1 Trust (Vishwas): the Foundation Value
- **Definition:** being assured that the other person *naturally wants my happiness and prosperity*.
- **The core error we make:**
  - We judge **ourselves by our intention** ("I mean well, so I am good").
  - We judge **others by their competence** ("they made a mistake, so they are bad").
- **Doubting intention** leads to irritation, anger and opposition.
- **Trusting intention** keeps the relationship continuous. If the other person lacks competence, we respond with *responsibility*, helping them improve instead of reacting against them.

> **Remember:** Intention and competence are different things. Judge intention (which is usually good) separately from competence (which can be improved).

### 3.2 Respect (Samman): Right Evaluation
- **Definition:** evaluating a person **correctly** (*Samyak Mapan*).

**Three forms of disrespect (wrong evaluation)**

| Type | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Over-evaluation** (Adhimulyan) | Evaluating *more* than what it is | Treating someone as superior because of their post or wealth |
| **Under-evaluation** (Avamulyan) | Evaluating *less* than what it is | Looking down on someone |
| **Otherwise evaluation** (Amulyan) | Evaluating as *something else* | Calling a human being a donkey |

**Differentiation vs. Respect**
- Society today **confuses differentiation with respect**. It bases "respect" on:
  - **Body:** gender, race, age, physical strength
  - **Physical facilities:** wealth, post
  - **Beliefs:** isms, sects
- Differentiation produces **opposition, movements, generation gaps and conflict**.
- **Real respect** is based on the **Self (I, the conscious entity)**. Every human being has the same **Purpose, Program and Potential**, and our different **competencies make us complementary** rather than competitors.

### 3.3 Affection (Sneha)
- The feeling of **being related** to the other.
- It follows naturally from Trust and Respect.

### 3.4 Care (Mamata) and Guidance (Vatsalya)

| Value | Responsibility towards a relative's... |
| --- | --- |
| **Care (Mamata)** | **Body**: nurturing and protecting it |
| **Guidance (Vatsalya)** | **Self (I)**: ensuring Right Understanding and Right Feeling |

### 3.5 Reverence (Shraddha)
- **Acceptance of excellence** in another person.
- **Excellence vs. being special**
  - *Excellence:* living in harmony **and helping others reach the same level**.
  - *Being special:* competitive; seeks to dominate or hoard.

### 3.6 Glory (Gaurav) and Gratitude (Kritagyata)

| Value | Feeling for... |
| --- | --- |
| **Glory (Gaurav)** | Those who have made efforts for **excellence** |
| **Gratitude (Kritagyata)** | Those who have made efforts for **my** excellence |

### 3.7 Love (Prem): the Complete Value
- The feeling of being **related to all human beings and all units in existence**.
- It is the foundation of an **Undivided Society (Akhand Samaj)**.

## 4. Society: Present State vs. Universal Order

## CO4 Quick Revision
- Family = mutual fulfillment; first place to practice harmony.
- **Trust** is the foundation. Judge intention and competence separately.
- **Respect** = right evaluation. Do not confuse it with differentiation by body, wealth or belief.
- Chain of values: Trust, Respect, Affection, Care/Guidance, Reverence, Glory/Gratitude, **Love**.
- Society, Crowd and Battlefield differ by the level of mutual fulfillment.
