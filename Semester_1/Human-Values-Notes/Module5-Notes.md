# CO5: Gender, Sensitization and Sexual Harassment

## 1. Sex vs. Gender

| Aspect | **Sex** | **Gender** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nature | Biological and physiological | Socially constructed |
| Based on | Anatomy (reproductive organs), chromosomes (XX, XY), genes, hormones (estrogen, testosterone) | Roles, behaviors, expectations, norms and identities |
| Categories | Male, female, intersex | Man, woman, non-binary |
| Origin | Natural; assigned at birth | Sociocultural and psychological; **learned** |
| Changeability | Largely fixed | Evolves across cultures and time |

- **Gender identity:** an individual's deeply felt **internal sense of self**. Learned behaviors and societal expectations shape gender roles and identity.
- **Gender studies:** an interdisciplinary field (psychology, sociology, history, political science). It examines how culture and power structures enforce norms of femininity and masculinity, and works towards gender equality and against discrimination.

## 2. Key Terminology

| Term | Meaning | Example / Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gender roles** | Behaviors, attitudes and responsibilities expected of people in public and private spheres based on their assigned sex | Expecting women to manage the household |
| **Gender stereotyping** | Assigning roles or behaviors based on preconceived prejudice | "Women are submissive, men are aggressive" |
| **Gender bias** | Preferential or differential treatment based on real or perceived gender | Unequal treatment at the workplace |
| **Gender discrimination** | Unequal or disadvantageous treatment based on gender or gender expression | Pay disparity, restricted education, misgendered speech |
| **Gender equity** | Being **fair** to all genders through targeted measures that recognize unique needs | Different support to reach equal outcomes |
| **Gender gap** | Disparity between men and women in economic participation, salary, political representation, education or culture | A measurable indicator |
| **Intersectionality** | Overlap of social identities (race, class, sexual orientation, disability) creating compounded discrimination or privilege | A disabled woman from a marginalized class faces layered disadvantage |

> **Equity vs. Equality:** equality means treating everyone the same. Equity means treating people *fairly according to their needs* so that outcomes become equal.

## 3. Gender Sensitization

**Definition:** the process of **creating awareness, examining personal beliefs, and changing stereotyped mindsets** that see men and women as unequal.

**Primary aims**
1. **Attitudinal change:** change age-old traditional views and break rigid stereotypes.
2. **Shift in focus:** gender is not only about "women"; it is about *people* and human equality.
3. **Education and awareness:** sensitize adolescent girls and boys to existing inequalities and gender-based violence.
4. **Inclusivity:** promote peaceful co-existence, mutual respect and dignified lives for all.

### Challenges in achieving gender equality
- Gender-based violence
- Pay gap
- Limited representation

## 6. Sexual Harassment

### 6.1 Definition
**Unwanted behavior that is sexual in nature.**

### 6.2 Four forms

| Form | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Verbal** | Unwanted comments or jokes |
| **Physical** | Unwanted touching or gestures |
| **Visual** | Offensive images or displays |
| **Cyber** | Harassment online or through technology platforms |

### 6.3 Recognizing everyday harassment
- **Unwanted attention:** persisting with uninvited advances or commentary
- **Inappropriate touching:** unwanted physical contact or gestures
- **Offensive jokes:** suggestive or demeaning humor
- **Stalking:** repeated, unwanted contact or communication

### 6.4 Consent and healthy relationships
A healthy relationship rests on **clear, enthusiastic agreement, free from coercion.**

| Pillar | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Respect** | Actively valuing others' boundaries |
| **Communication** | Open and honest conversation |
| **Equality** | Keeping power dynamics balanced |

### 6.5 Bystander intervention (the 4 Ds/actions)

| Action | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Speak up** | Challenge the behavior directly |
| **Distract** | Change the subject or create a diversion |
| **Support** | Offer help and comfort to the victim |
| **Report** | Inform the appropriate authorities |

### 6.6 Coping strategies for victims
1. **Talk** to a trusted friend for emotional support.
2. **Seek professional help** (counseling or therapy).
3. **Document** the harassment: exact dates, times, and details.
4. **Report** officially to a manager, supervisor or HR.

### 6.7 Institutional prevention and culture

| Policy element | Associated procedure |
| --- | --- |
| Non-discrimination policy | Clear reporting process for complaints |
| Harassment prevention training | Available resources and support services |

**Building a culture of respect**
- **Open communication:** encourage honest dialogue
- **Training and education:** promote awareness and mutual understanding
- **Zero-tolerance policy:** clear, uncompromised consequences for harassment

- Bystander: **Speak up, Distract, Support, Report.**
