## TOPIC 1: HUMAN BEING AS A CO-EXISTENCE OF 'I' AND 'BODY'

### 1.1 Introduction
- A human being = co-existence of two distinct entities: **Self ('I')** and **Body**.
- Both are **fundamentally different** in nature, needs, and activities — yet work together in close relationship.

### 1.2 Comparison Table (very high-yield — memorize this row by row)

| Feature | Self ('I') | Body |
|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | Sentient (conscious, non-physical) | Material (physico-chemical, physical) |
| **Needs** | Happiness (Sukh) — continuous | Physical Facilities (Suvidha) — temporary |
| **Time Scale** | Continuous / Permanent | Temporary (has a life span) |
| **Means of Fulfillment** | Right Understanding & Right Feeling | Physical Facilities |
| **Activities** | Desiring, thinking, imagining, knowing | Eating, breathing, walking, growing |

> **Exam anchor phrase:** *"'I' is Sentient, Body is Material — 'I' wants Happiness (continuous), Body wants Facility (temporary)."*

## TOPIC 2: NEEDS OF THE SELF AND THE BODY

### 2.1 Needs of the Self ('I') — Happiness (Sukh)
- **Qualitative** — about feeling respected, trusted, happy.
- **Continuous in time** — we want it *permanently*, not intermittently.

### 2.2 Needs of the Body — Physical Facilities (Suvidha)
- **Quantitative** — needed in a *limited/finite* quantity (e.g., food only up to a point).
- **Temporary in time** — needed *periodically*, not continuously.

### Comparison Snapshot

| Aspect | Happiness (Self) | Physical Facility (Body) |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Qualitative | Quantitative |
| Quantity needed | Not applicable (a feeling) | Limited/finite |
| Time needed | Continuous | Periodic/Temporary |

> **Mnemonic:** **Self = Sukh = Sentient = Steady (continuous)**; **Body = Suvidha = Substance = Sometimes (temporary)**

## TOPIC 3: THE BODY AS AN INSTRUMENT OF 'I'

| Role of 'I' | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **I am the Seer** | Eyes don't see by themselves — the Self sees *through* the eyes |
| **I am the Doer** | The Self decides the action, then uses hands/legs/body parts to execute it |
| **I am the Enjoyer** | Body only receives the *touch*; the Self experiences the *taste and enjoyment* |

> **Key exam line:** *"The Body acts as an instrument of the Self ('I')."*
> The body senses/executes; the **'I' interprets, decides, and enjoys**.

## TOPIC 4: ACTIVITIES AND HARMONY IN THE SELF ('I')

### 4.1 Four Continuous Activities of the Self

| # | Activity | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Imaging** | Creating desires, pictures, or expectations |
| 2 | **Analyzing** | Breaking down thoughts, weighing options |
| 3 | **Selecting / Tasting** | Choosing what aligns with desires; experiencing the "taste" of things |
| 4 | **Realization & Understanding** | Gaining absolute clarity and right understanding |

> **Mnemonic:** **I-A-S-R** → *Imaging, Analyzing, Selecting, Realization*

### 4.2 Harmony in the Self
- Harmony in 'I' = **no contradiction** between desires, thoughts, and expectations.
- Achieved through **Right Understanding**.

> **One-liner:** *Harmony in 'I' = Right Understanding → no internal contradiction.*

## TOPIC 5: HARMONY OF 'I' WITH THE BODY — SANYAM AND HEALTH

### 5.1 Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sanyam (Self-regulation)** | The feeling of **responsibility** in 'I' for **nurturing, protecting, and rightly utilizing** the Body |
| **Health (Swasthya)** | State of the Body where **every part performs its expected function correctly** |

> **Key distinction:** **Sanyam** is a responsibility that lies in the **Self** (towards the body); **Health** is a *state* of the **Body itself**.

### 5.2 Programs to Ensure Sanyam and Health

**For Sanyam:**
- Right understanding about the body
- Consuming physical facilities only **as necessary** — not out of greed or taste alone

**For Health (4 pillars — commonly asked as a list):**
1. **Proper intake** — nutritious food & water
2. **Proper labour (श्रम)** — physical exercise
3. **Proper rest & regulation**
4. **Protection** from adverse elements + **right treatment** when ill (avoiding chemical misuse)

> **Mnemonic for Health pillars:** **I-L-R-P** → *Intake, Labour, Rest, Protection*
