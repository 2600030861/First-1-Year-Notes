# Japanese (JLPT N5 Level) – Detailed Syllabus

## Overview

| Unit | Focus | What you can do by the end |
|------|-------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Kanji & Vocabulary | Read and write about 80 basic Kanji; use essential everyday vocabulary |
| 2 | Core Grammar | Build sentences, conjugate verbs, use adjectives, particles and the て-form |
| 3 | Expressing Yourself | Talk about daily life, preferences, abilities, experiences, invitations, suggestions and opinions |
| 4 | The Four Skills | Read, listen, speak and write on familiar topics at JLPT N5 level |

**Target level:** JLPT N5
