## Course Overview

This course focuses on understanding **human values, relationships, social harmony, professional ethics, gender equality, moral reasoning, and responsible living**.

The course aims to develop:

* Self-awareness and self-exploration
* Understanding of happiness and prosperity
* Harmony within the self and family
* Social and environmental responsibility
* Professional and engineering ethics
* Gender sensitivity and equality
* Ethical decision-making
* Moral courage and responsible leadership

## 14. Equity vs Equality

### Equality

Providing everyone with the same opportunities or resources.

### Equity

Recognising different circumstances and providing appropriate support to achieve fair outcomes.

```text
Equality → Same treatment

Equity → Fair treatment based on needs and circumstances
```

# UNIT 5 — Moral Theories & Ethical Decision-Making

## 1. Moral Theories

Moral theories provide frameworks for evaluating whether actions are ethically right or wrong.

---

## 2. Consequentialism

* Focuses on consequences of actions
* An action is evaluated based on its outcomes
* Greatest-good reasoning
* Benefits and harms

### Key Question

> What will be the consequences of this action?

---

## 3. Deontology

* Duty-based ethics
* Focuses on rules, duties, and principles
* Respect for moral obligations
* Certain actions may be wrong even if they produce good outcomes

### Key Question

> Is this action consistent with my duties and moral principles?

---

## 4. Virtue Ethics

* Focuses on character
* Development of good moral qualities
* Courage
* Honesty
* Compassion
* Justice
* Responsibility

### Key Question

> What would a good and responsible person do?

# Ethical Decision-Making

## 7. Ethical Decision-Making Frameworks

A general ethical decision-making process:

```text
Identify the ethical issue
          ↓
Gather relevant facts
          ↓
Identify stakeholders
          ↓
Identify possible options
          ↓
Evaluate ethical consequences
          ↓
Apply ethical principles
          ↓
Choose the most responsible action
          ↓
Review the outcome
```

---

## 8. Resolving Moral Dilemmas
